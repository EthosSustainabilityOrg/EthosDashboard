## **1\. Brand Overview**

Ethos Sustainability is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to bringing sustainable STEAM and business education to youth around the world. Our brand should consistently reflect:

* Professionalism and credibility.  
* Youth leadership and innovation.  
* A clear focus on sustainability, education, and community impact.

These guidelines apply to **all chapters** and must be followed whenever using the Ethos Sustainability name, logo, or visual identity.

## **2\. Logo Usage**

## **2.1 Primary Logo**

* Use the primary Ethos Sustainability logo on:  
  * Flyers and posters.  
  * Presentation slides.  
  * Websites and social media graphics.  
  * Event signage and formal documents.

**Rules:**

* Do not stretch, distort, or rotate the logo.  
* Do not change the colors of the logo.  
* Do not add shadows, outlines, or extra effects.  
* Do not place the logo over busy backgrounds that make it hard to read.

Leave enough clear space around the logo (at least the height of the "E" in "Ethos") so it does not feel crowded by other text or images.

## **2.2 Chapter Lockup**

When naming a chapter, use the following format:

* **Ethos Sustainability – \[School Name\] Chapter**

Examples:

* Ethos Sustainability – TAMS Chapter  
* Ethos Sustainability – UT Dallas Chapter

If you use a lockup (logo \+ chapter name):

* Place the chapter name **below or to the right** of the primary logo, in the approved font and color.  
* Do not modify the logo itself to squeeze the chapter name inside.

## **3\. Colors**

## **3.1 Primary Colors**

* **Ethos Espresso** – used for main accents, headings, and buttons.  
  * HEX: \#413429  
  * RGB: 65, 52, 41  
* **Ethos Peach**  – used for body text and strong headings.  
  * HEX: \#FCBD9D  
  * RGB: 252, 189, 157

## **3.2 Secondary / Accent Colors**

* **Ethos Cream / Accent** – used for page backgrounds and light section fills.  
  * HEX: \#FFFBF4  
  * RGB: 255, 251, 244  
* **Ethos Warm Gray** – used for borders, lines, and secondary text.  
  * HEX: \#A89E94  
  * RGB: 168, 158, 148  
* **Ethos Sand** – subtle dividers, card backgrounds, and hover states.  
  * HEX: \#D4CCC4  
  * RGB: 212, 204, 196  
* **Ethos Peach Light** – soft backgrounds behind featured content.  
  * HEX: \#FFE2D6  
  * RGB: 255, 226, 214  
* **Ethos Brown Mid** – subheadings and supporting text.  
  * HEX: \#7D6F63  
  * RGB: 125, 111, 99  
* **Ethos Brown Dark** – alternative to Espresso for body text.  
  * HEX: \#514033  
  * RGB: 81, 64, 51

## **3.3 Usage Guidelines**

* **Use Ethos Espresso for:**  
  * Titles, section headers, and strong headings  
  * Body text throughout documents  
  * Any text that must remain highly legible on light backgrounds  
* **Use Ethos Peach for:**  
  * Highlight boxes and call-to-action banners  
  * Decorative accents, dividers, and graphic elements  
  * Color blocks behind pull quotes or featured content  
* **Use Ethos Cream for:**  
  * Page backgrounds and section fills  
  * Text placed on Espresso-colored backgrounds or dark shapes  
* **Use Ethos Warm Gray for:**  
  * Captions, footnotes, and secondary labels  
  * Table borders, rule lines, and dividers  
  * Supporting text that should recede visually behind primary content

Avoid using too many different colors in a single piece. Stick to **2–3 brand colors** at a time for a clean, professional look.

## **4\. Typography (Fonts)**

Until a specific font system is finalized, use this structure:

* **Headings (Titles / Section Headers)**  
  * Recommended: Georgia  
  * Weight: Bold or Semi-Bold.  
  * Color: Ethos Dark or Ethos Green.  
* **Body Text**  
  * Recommended: Times New Roman or EB Garamond (Semi Bold)  
  * Weight: Regular.  
  * Color: Ethos Dark.  
* **Captions / Footnotes**  
  * Same as body text, slightly smaller, in Ethos Dark or Neutral Gray.

Chapters should **not** use novelty or cartoon fonts (e.g., Comic Sans) on official Ethos materials, unless used for a purpose such as activities.

## **5\. Tone and Messaging**

All Ethos Sustainability chapter communications should be:

* **Formal and to the point.**  
* **Clear, educational, and accurate.**  
* **Inclusive and welcoming.**  
* **Aligned with our mission:** sustainable STEAM \+ business education for youth.

When describing Ethos, chapters should use an approved short description such as:

Ethos Sustainability is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to bringing sustainable STEAM and business education to youth. We host conferences, workshops, and projects that help students apply sustainability concepts to real-world challenges in their schools and communities.

Chapters may add a sentence about their local focus, but should maintain the core description.

## **6\. Approved Uses of the Brand**

Chapters may use the Ethos Sustainability name and logo for:

* Official chapter meetings and events.  
* Educational workshops and conferences related to sustainability, STEAM, and business.  
* Recruitment materials (flyers, social media posts, info sessions).  
* Co-branded materials with **school-approved** partners that align with Ethos values and have received HQ approval.

All materials should:

* Use the correct chapter name format.  
* Use the approved logo and colors.  
* Reflect Ethos Sustainability's mission and values (no discrimination, educational focus, respect for minors and vulnerable groups).

## **7\. Restricted Uses and Approvals**

To protect the organization and maintain consistency, chapters **must not**:

* Alter the logo or create new versions of the brand mark.  
* Use the Ethos name or logo on:  
  * Contracts, MOUs, or legal agreements without HQ approval.  
  * Sponsorship materials, fundraising campaigns, or grant applications without HQ approval.  
  * Partner materials where Ethos appears alongside external brands that **have not** been approved by HQ.

The following always require **HQ approval** before use:

* Major sponsorship or partnership logos displayed with Ethos.  
* Media materials (press releases, interviews representing Ethos).  
* Use of the logo on merchandise (shirts, hoodies, stickers, etc.).  
* Any national, regional, or multi-school event branding.

When in doubt, chapters should send a draft to HQ via Slack or email for review before publishing.

## **8\. Accessibility and Inclusion**

Chapters must ensure that their materials are not only on-brand, but also accessible and inclusive:

* Use high contrast between text and background (e.g., dark text on light background).  
* Avoid using color as the only way to convey information.  
* Use clear, readable font sizes (no tiny text in flyers or slides).  
* Use inclusive language and images that reflect diverse students and communities.

## **9\. Contact for Brand Questions**

If a chapter is unsure whether a design, logo usage, or partnership is appropriate:

* Contact Ethos Sustainability HQ via the designated Slack channel (e.g., \#brand-approvals or \#chapter-leads).  
* Or email: brand@ethossustainbility.org.

Chapters should wait for approval before printing or publishing materials that significantly feature the Ethos brand.
