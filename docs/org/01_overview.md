**Who We Are**  
Ethos Sustainability is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to bringing sustainable STEAM and business education to youth around the world. We are a youth-led, international organization that believes students do not have to wait to lead— they can start now by learning to build a more sustainable future through science, technology, engineering, arts, mathematics, and entrepreneurship.

**What We Do**  
We design and deliver hands-on educational experiences that make sustainability real, practical, and actionable for young people. Our work includes conferences, workshops, and project-based programs that connect sustainable concepts with real-world challenges in business and STEAM fields. Chapters and HQ work together to reach K–12 and higher education communities, helping students explore topics such as renewable energy, the circular economy, climate solutions, and sustainable entrepreneurship.

**Our Chapter Model**  
Ethos Sustainability chapters are school-based clubs at high schools and colleges that extend our mission into local communities. Each chapter:

* Runs monthly events or activities focused on sustainable STEAM and business education.  
* Organizes local workshops and projects that address sustainability challenges in their school or community.  
* Collaborates with HQ on major initiatives, including regional conferences, multi-school events, and flagship programs.

Chapters operate with significant support and direction from HQ. All major projects, budgets, sponsorships, and co-hosted events are approved by HQ to maintain quality, alignment with our mission, and legal and financial compliance.

**How We Handle Impact and Finances**  
Ethos Sustainability maintains a centralized financial structure to keep all chapter activities transparent and accountable. Chapters may collect dues and donations, but all funds are directed into the corporate HQ account and tracked through standardized project proposals, budgets, and post-event reports. This ensures:

* Clear documentation of how money is raised and spent.  
* Consistent legal and safety practices, especially when working with minors.  
* The ability to scale nationally and internationally while protecting our mission and reputation.

Each event or project is expected to include a simple plan, a focus on learning outcomes, and a post-event report covering attendance, feedback, and finances. Semester summaries from chapters help HQ understand the overall impact and provide better support.

**Our Values and Expectations**  
Ethos Sustainability is committed to creating inclusive, safe, and equitable learning environments. Chapters must:

* Welcome members without discrimination of any kind.  
* Maintain active participation through regular meetings, monthly events, and engagement with HQ.  
* Follow HQ policies on branding, finances, media, and partnerships.

We expect every chapter to "do something" meaningful with sustainability, STEAM, and entrepreneurship— whether that's a workshop, a community project, a student-led conference, or a collaborative event with HQ.

**How Chapters and HQ Work Together**  
Our model is intentionally hybrid: HQ sets direction, provides curriculum, tools, and training, and leads major initiatives, while chapters act as local engines of engagement and action. When HQ hosts events in a region, nearby chapters help recruit participants, support logistics, and contribute volunteers and student leaders. In turn, HQ provides chapters with resources, coaching, and a global network of peers.

Together, Ethos Sustainability and its chapters aim to build a generation of young people who understand sustainability and know how to apply it in STEAM and business— turning awareness into real impact in their schools, communities, and future careers.
