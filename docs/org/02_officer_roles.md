# **Purpose**

The success of every Ethos Sustainability Chapter depends on the leadership and collaboration of its officers. While each officer has specific responsibilities, all officers work together to support the chapter's mission, serve its members, and create meaningful educational opportunities within their school and community.

This document outlines the purpose, responsibilities, and expectations of each officer position. It is designed to help both current and future chapter leaders understand their role and confidently fulfill their leadership responsibilities.

Additional information about chapter governance can be found in the **Chapter Bylaws** and **Chapter Charter & Affiliation Agreement**.

---

# **Overview**

Serving as a chapter officer is an opportunity to develop leadership, communication, organization, and problem-solving skills while making a positive impact in your community.

Every officer contributes to the chapter in a unique way. Some roles focus on planning events, while others emphasize communication, finances, recordkeeping, or member engagement. Although responsibilities are divided among officers, successful chapters are built on teamwork rather than individual effort.

No officer is expected to manage every aspect of the chapter alone. Officers should regularly communicate with one another, support fellow leaders when needed, and seek guidance from the Faculty Advisor or Ethos Sustainability Headquarters (HQ) whenever questions arise.

Leadership is not measured by the number of tasks completed—it is measured by the positive experiences created for members and the impact the chapter has on its community.

---

# **Leadership Philosophy**

Ethos Sustainability believes that effective leadership is built on service, collaboration, and accountability.

Chapter officers are expected to lead by example by demonstrating professionalism, respect, integrity, and a commitment to the organization's mission. They should encourage participation, listen to new ideas, and create an environment where every member feels welcomed and valued.

Strong leaders recognize that leadership is not about having all the answers. It is about bringing people together, supporting others, solving challenges collaboratively, and continuously learning.

As representatives of Ethos Sustainability, chapter officers should strive to reflect the organization's values both during chapter activities and in their interactions with members, school staff, community partners, and Headquarters.

# **President**

## **Purpose of the Role**

The President serves as the primary student leader of the chapter and is responsible for coordinating the overall operation of the organization at the local level.

The President provides direction for the chapter, helps establish goals, supports the officer team, and serves as the primary point of contact between the chapter and Ethos Sustainability Headquarters.

While the President provides leadership, they are not expected to complete every task independently. Their primary responsibility is to guide, organize, and support the work of the entire leadership team.

---

## **Primary Responsibilities**

The President is responsible for helping ensure the chapter remains active, organized, and aligned with the mission of Ethos Sustainability.

Primary responsibilities include:

* Serving as the primary contact between the chapter and Headquarters.  
* Leading chapter meetings and officer meetings.  
* Working with officers to establish goals for the academic year.  
* Coordinating chapter activities and educational programming.  
* Ensuring required reports and documentation are completed on time.  
* Overseeing project planning and proposal development.  
* Working closely with the Faculty Advisor to support chapter operations.  
* Encouraging collaboration among officers and members.  
* Helping resolve challenges that arise within the chapter.  
* Representing the chapter during meetings with Headquarters, school administrators, and community partners when appropriate.

---

## **Leadership Expectations**

The President should focus on creating an environment where officers and members are empowered to contribute.

Effective Presidents:

* Communicate clearly and consistently.  
* Listen to feedback from members.  
* Delegate responsibilities rather than attempting to complete every task themselves.  
* Encourage teamwork.  
* Recognize the contributions of fellow officers and volunteers.  
* Maintain a positive and professional attitude.  
* Lead with integrity and respect.

Leadership is about supporting others and helping the entire chapter succeed.

---

## **Working With Headquarters**

The President serves as the primary communication link between the chapter and Headquarters.

Responsibilities include:

* Attending required Chapter Leader meetings.  
* Sharing important updates from Headquarters with chapter officers and members.  
* Asking questions whenever clarification is needed.  
* Submitting required organizational documents on behalf of the chapter when appropriate.  
* Coordinating with Headquarters regarding project approvals, organizational initiatives, and chapter support.

Maintaining regular communication helps ensure the chapter has access to resources, guidance, and organizational updates.

---

## **Working With the Faculty Advisor**

The President should maintain regular communication with the Faculty Advisor throughout the school year.

This partnership helps ensure that chapter activities remain consistent with school policies while providing students with mentorship and support.

The President should consult the Faculty Advisor regarding:

* Meeting schedules.  
* Event planning.  
* School approval requirements.  
* Volunteer coordination.  
* Facility reservations.  
* Questions related to school procedures.

Building a strong relationship with the Faculty Advisor contributes significantly to the long-term success of the chapter.

---

## **Working With Other Officers**

The President works closely with every member of the officer team.

Examples include:

* Collaborating with the Vice President to plan meetings and events.  
* Working with the Secretary to review agendas, attendance records, and meeting minutes.  
* Coordinating with the Treasurer during project planning and budget development.  
* Supporting the Outreach Coordinator in recruitment efforts and community partnerships.  
* Assisting optional officers as additional programs or initiatives are developed.

The President's role is to coordinate these efforts rather than manage every detail personally.

---

## **Time Commitment**

The time required to serve as President varies depending on the chapter's size and activities.

On average, Presidents should expect to dedicate several hours each week to responsibilities such as:

* Preparing for meetings.  
* Communicating with officers.  
* Responding to emails and messages.  
* Planning upcoming events.  
* Meeting with the Faculty Advisor.  
* Participating in Headquarters meetings.  
* Reviewing chapter progress.

Time commitments may increase before major events, conferences, or large community projects.

# **Vice President**

## **Purpose of the Role**

The Vice President supports the President in leading the chapter and helps ensure that chapter operations continue smoothly throughout the academic year. This position serves as a key member of the leadership team by assisting with planning, coordinating chapter activities, and helping officers achieve the chapter's goals.

The Vice President should be prepared to assume additional responsibilities when the President is unavailable and help maintain continuity within the chapter.

---

## **Primary Responsibilities**

The Vice President is responsible for supporting chapter leadership and assisting with the successful execution of chapter activities.

Primary responsibilities include:

* Assisting the President with chapter operations and planning.  
* Helping organize chapter meetings and events.  
* Supporting the development of educational programs and activities.  
* Monitoring the progress of chapter projects.  
* Assisting officers with completing assigned responsibilities.  
* Serving as acting President when necessary.  
* Helping coordinate officer meetings and planning sessions.  
* Contributing ideas that support chapter growth and member engagement.

---

## **Role Expectations**

The Vice President is expected to:

* Remain informed about all chapter activities.  
* Communicate regularly with the President and other officers.  
* Be prepared to assist wherever additional support is needed.  
* Help ensure projects remain organized and on schedule.  
* Represent Ethos Sustainability professionally during meetings and events.

A successful Vice President is flexible, dependable, and willing to support the needs of the chapter.

---

## **Working With Headquarters**

While the President serves as the primary point of contact, the Vice President should remain familiar with organizational updates and expectations.

The Vice President may:

* Attend meetings with Headquarters when appropriate.  
* Assist with organizational reporting.  
* Support the President in implementing initiatives from Headquarters.  
* Communicate with Headquarters when delegated by the President.

---

## **Working With the Faculty Advisor**

The Vice President should work alongside the President and Faculty Advisor to ensure chapter activities comply with school policies and remain organized.

The Vice President may also assist with coordinating logistics for meetings, workshops, and special events.

---

## **Working With Other Officers**

The Vice President collaborates closely with every officer to help coordinate chapter activities.

This includes:

* Assisting the Secretary with meeting preparation.  
* Working with the Treasurer during event planning.  
* Supporting the Outreach Coordinator with recruitment initiatives.  
* Helping optional officers coordinate programs and special projects.

Strong communication and teamwork are essential to this role.

---

## **Time Commitment**

The Vice President should expect to dedicate several hours each week to chapter responsibilities, including attending meetings, supporting event planning, communicating with officers, and assisting with ongoing projects.

Additional time may be required before major events or when serving in the President's absence.

# **Secretary**

## **Purpose of the Role**

The Secretary is responsible for maintaining accurate chapter records and supporting effective communication within the leadership team.

By documenting meetings, tracking attendance, and organizing important information, the Secretary helps ensure continuity between meetings and leadership transitions.

Accurate recordkeeping allows the chapter to remain organized and provides valuable documentation for future officers.

---

## **Primary Responsibilities**

The Secretary is responsible for maintaining the chapter's official records.

Primary responsibilities include:

* Recording meeting minutes.  
* Maintaining attendance records.  
* Preparing meeting agendas in collaboration with the President.  
* Organizing chapter documents and files.  
* Maintaining an accurate calendar of meetings and events.  
* Tracking action items assigned during meetings.  
* Assisting with organizational documentation when requested.

---

## **Role Expectations**

The Secretary is expected to:

* Produce clear and accurate meeting records.  
* Keep chapter documentation organized and accessible.  
* Maintain confidentiality when appropriate.  
* Submit meeting records promptly following meetings.  
* Support officers by maintaining accurate organizational information.

Reliable documentation helps ensure that important decisions and responsibilities are not overlooked.

---

## **Working With Headquarters**

The Secretary may assist with preparing documentation requested by Headquarters, including meeting records, attendance information, or organizational reports.

The Secretary should also help maintain copies of important organizational documents provided by Headquarters.

---

## **Working With the Faculty Advisor**

The Secretary may coordinate with the Faculty Advisor regarding meeting schedules, school documentation requirements, and organizational records when appropriate.

---

## **Working With Other Officers**

The Secretary supports every officer by maintaining accurate records of chapter activities.

Examples include:

* Recording officer meeting discussions.  
* Tracking responsibilities assigned during planning sessions.  
* Maintaining documentation related to chapter events.  
* Organizing files needed by future leadership teams.

---

## **Time Commitment**

The Secretary should expect to dedicate several hours each week to recording meetings, organizing documents, updating chapter records, and preparing materials for upcoming meetings.

Time commitments may increase during periods of active event planning or leadership transitions.

---

# **Treasurer**

## **Purpose of the Role**

The Treasurer is responsible for supporting the financial planning and documentation of chapter activities in accordance with the financial policies established by Ethos Sustainability Headquarters.

This role promotes accountability by helping the chapter plan responsibly, maintain accurate financial records, and complete required financial documentation.

The Treasurer works closely with Headquarters, the President, and other officers to ensure financial information is accurate and submitted on time.

---

## **Primary Responsibilities**

The Treasurer supports the chapter's financial planning and reporting by:

* Preparing preliminary budgets for proposed events.  
* Tracking estimated and approved project expenses.  
* Maintaining financial records related to chapter activities.  
* Assisting with required financial reports.  
* Keeping organized records of receipts and financial documentation.  
* Monitoring project budgets throughout implementation.  
* Working with the President during project proposal development.

---

## **Role Expectations**

The Treasurer is expected to:

* Maintain organized financial records.  
* Handle financial information responsibly and accurately.  
* Communicate budget concerns promptly.  
* Support transparent financial reporting.  
* Follow all organizational financial policies established by Headquarters.

Accuracy and organization are essential to fulfilling this role successfully.

---

## **Working With Headquarters**

The Treasurer regularly works with Headquarters regarding financial procedures and documentation.

Responsibilities may include:

* Assisting with budget submissions.  
* Providing financial information for organizational reports.  
* Reviewing approved budgets.  
* Asking questions regarding financial procedures when clarification is needed.

---

## **Working With the Faculty Advisor**

The Treasurer should communicate with the Faculty Advisor regarding school financial procedures when applicable and help ensure chapter activities follow both school and organizational expectations.

---

## **Working With Other Officers**

The Treasurer collaborates with all chapter officers during project planning.

Examples include:

* Working with the President to prepare project budgets.  
* Assisting the Vice President during event planning.  
* Coordinating with the Outreach Coordinator regarding sponsorship opportunities.  
* Supporting committees by providing budget information for approved projects.

Financial planning is most effective when completed collaboratively with the entire leadership team.

---

## **Time Commitment**

The Treasurer should expect to dedicate several hours each week to budget planning, financial documentation, report preparation, and supporting chapter events.

Workload may increase before major events, fundraising activities, or required financial reporting deadlines.

**Outreach Coordinator**

## **Purpose of the Role**

The Outreach Coordinator is responsible for helping the chapter grow by recruiting new members, promoting chapter activities, and building relationships within the school and local community. This role focuses on increasing awareness of Ethos Sustainability and encouraging participation in chapter programs and events.

The Outreach Coordinator plays an important role in ensuring that students, educators, and community members understand the mission of the organization and the opportunities available through the chapter.

---

## **Primary Responsibilities**

The Outreach Coordinator supports chapter growth through communication and community engagement.

Primary responsibilities include:

* Recruiting new members throughout the academic year.  
* Promoting chapter meetings, workshops, and events.  
* Assisting with member onboarding and engagement.  
* Building relationships with teachers, school staff, and student organizations.  
* Identifying opportunities to collaborate with community partners.  
* Supporting outreach efforts for conferences and organizational initiatives.  
* Helping distribute promotional materials approved by Headquarters.  
* Working with officers to increase participation in chapter activities.

---

## **Role Expectations**

The Outreach Coordinator is expected to:

* Communicate professionally with students, educators, and community members.  
* Represent Ethos Sustainability positively during recruitment efforts.  
* Encourage an inclusive and welcoming environment for prospective members.  
* Keep officers informed about outreach activities and partnership opportunities.  
* Support chapter goals by helping maintain consistent member engagement.

Successful outreach focuses on building meaningful relationships rather than simply increasing membership numbers.

---

## **Working With Headquarters**

The Outreach Coordinator may work with Headquarters regarding:

* Recruitment campaigns.  
* Approved marketing materials.  
* Community partnerships requiring organizational approval.  
* Promotional opportunities for organizational initiatives.  
* Questions regarding organizational branding or public communications.

When appropriate, Headquarters may provide outreach resources, templates, and promotional materials to support chapter recruitment efforts.

---

## **Working With the Faculty Advisor**

The Outreach Coordinator should communicate with the Faculty Advisor regarding school-approved methods for recruitment and event promotion.

The Faculty Advisor may also provide guidance on connecting with other student organizations, school departments, or community partners.

---

## **Working With Other Officers**

The Outreach Coordinator works closely with the entire officer team to promote chapter activities.

Examples include:

* Coordinating with the President to support chapter growth initiatives.  
* Assisting the Vice President with event promotion.  
* Working with the Secretary to communicate meeting information.  
* Collaborating with the Treasurer when fundraising or sponsorship activities require outreach.  
* Supporting optional officers in promoting educational programs and special events.

Effective outreach is most successful when communication is coordinated across the entire leadership team.

---

## **Time Commitment**

The Outreach Coordinator should expect to dedicate several hours each week to recruitment activities, communication efforts, event promotion, and relationship building.

Additional time may be required before recruitment events, chapter launches, conferences, or major educational programs.

# **Optional Officer Roles**

## **Purpose**

As chapters grow, additional leadership positions may help distribute responsibilities more effectively and provide more opportunities for members to develop leadership skills.

Optional officer positions are not required for chapter recognition. Instead, they may be created when they support the chapter's goals, programming, or organizational needs.

The responsibilities of optional officers should be clearly defined by the chapter and included in the chapter's governing documents when appropriate.

---

## **Media Lead**

### **Purpose of the Role**

The Media Lead is responsible for supporting the chapter's visual communications and digital presence.

Depending on the chapter's activities, responsibilities may include:

* Taking photographs at chapter events.  
* Creating graphics using approved organizational branding.  
* Assisting with social media content.  
* Helping document chapter activities.  
* Organizing media files for future use.  
* Supporting promotional campaigns approved by Headquarters.

All public communications should follow Ethos Sustainability's branding guidelines and any applicable school policies.

---

## **Curriculum Lead**

### **Purpose of the Role**

The Curriculum Lead supports the educational mission of the chapter by helping prepare and organize workshops, presentations, and learning activities.

Responsibilities may include:

* Preparing educational materials.  
* Assisting workshop facilitators.  
* Organizing lesson resources.  
* Helping adapt activities for different age groups.  
* Coordinating educational supplies for chapter programs.  
* Supporting educational initiatives developed by Headquarters.

The Curriculum Lead works closely with officers responsible for event planning and educational programming.

---

## **Additional Optional Positions**

Every chapter is unique. Depending on chapter size, school requirements, and programming, additional officer positions may be established to support chapter operations.

Examples include:

* Events Coordinator  
* Volunteer Coordinator  
* Membership Coordinator  
* Technology Coordinator  
* Conference Coordinator  
* Partnerships Coordinator

Before creating additional officer positions, chapters should consider whether the responsibilities are ongoing, clearly defined, and beneficial to the chapter's long-term success.

# **Officer Collaboration**

## **Working as One Leadership Team**

Although each officer has individual responsibilities, the chapter is most successful when officers work collaboratively toward shared goals.

Open communication, mutual respect, and a willingness to support one another create a stronger leadership team and contribute to a more positive experience for chapter members.

Officers should regularly communicate about:

* Upcoming meetings.  
* Event planning.  
* Project timelines.  
* Member engagement.  
* Organizational updates.  
* Challenges requiring additional support.

No officer should feel responsible for managing the chapter alone. Leadership responsibilities should be shared whenever possible.

---

## **Communication Expectations**

Effective communication helps chapters remain organized and prevents misunderstandings.

Officer teams are encouraged to:

* Respond to messages in a timely manner.  
* Share important updates with fellow officers.  
* Notify the team when challenges arise.  
* Attend scheduled officer meetings whenever possible.  
* Maintain respectful and professional communication.

When questions involve organizational policies or approvals, officers should communicate with Headquarters rather than making assumptions.

---

## **Supporting One Another**

Every leadership team experiences busy periods throughout the school year. Officers are encouraged to assist one another when additional support is needed.

Examples include:

* Helping prepare for events.  
* Assisting with meeting setup.  
* Supporting recruitment activities.  
* Sharing responsibilities during officer absences.  
* Collaborating on project planning.

Working together strengthens both the leadership team and the chapter as a whole.

---

## **Shared Responsibility**

While officers have different responsibilities, all officers share responsibility for the overall success of the chapter.

Every officer should contribute to:

* Maintaining a positive chapter culture.  
* Supporting the mission of Ethos Sustainability.  
* Encouraging member participation.  
* Representing the organization professionally.  
* Helping create meaningful educational opportunities.

Strong chapters are built through collaboration, accountability, and a shared commitment to serving members and the community.

# **Officer Expectations**

## **Commitment to Leadership**

Serving as a chapter officer is a commitment to supporting both the chapter and the mission of Ethos Sustainability. Officers are expected to fulfill the responsibilities of their position, work collaboratively with fellow leaders, and contribute to creating a positive experience for all members.

Leadership requires reliability, communication, organization, and a willingness to serve others. Officers should strive to complete assigned responsibilities on time, communicate proactively, and seek assistance whenever challenges arise.

---

## **Professional Conduct**

Chapter officers represent both their local chapter and Ethos Sustainability.

Officers are expected to:

* Demonstrate honesty and integrity.  
* Treat members, advisors, and community partners with respect.  
* Maintain professional communication.  
* Follow school policies and organizational expectations.  
* Support an inclusive and welcoming chapter environment.  
* Address disagreements respectfully and constructively.

Professional conduct helps build trust within the chapter and strengthens relationships with schools, families, and community partners.

---

## **Active Participation**

Leadership extends beyond holding a title. Officers are expected to actively participate in chapter operations throughout their term.

This includes:

* Attending chapter meetings.  
* Participating in officer meetings.  
* Assisting with chapter events.  
* Completing assigned responsibilities.  
* Communicating with fellow officers.  
* Supporting organizational initiatives when appropriate.

Consistent participation contributes to a stronger and more effective leadership team.

---

## **Communication**

Clear communication is one of the most important responsibilities shared by all officers.

Officers should communicate regularly with:

* Fellow officers.  
* Chapter members.  
* The Faculty Advisor.  
* Ethos Sustainability Headquarters, when appropriate.

Keeping stakeholders informed helps prevent misunderstandings and ensures chapter activities remain organized.

# **Leadership Transitions**

## **Preparing Future Leaders**

Leadership transitions are a natural part of every student organization. As officers graduate or complete their terms, new leaders should receive the information and resources they need to continue the chapter's success.

A thoughtful transition helps preserve institutional knowledge and allows the incoming leadership team to begin their term with confidence.

---

## **Responsibilities of Outgoing Officers**

Before leaving office, outgoing officers should make every reasonable effort to prepare their successors by:

* Sharing important documents and files.  
* Explaining ongoing projects and upcoming deadlines.  
* Providing access to approved organizational resources.  
* Reviewing responsibilities associated with the position.  
* Answering questions from incoming officers.  
* Assisting with the transition when possible.

These efforts help ensure continuity from one leadership team to the next.

---

## **Responsibilities of Incoming Officers**

Incoming officers should take an active role in preparing for their new responsibilities.

This includes:

* Reviewing applicable Chapter Starter Kit documents.  
* Meeting with outgoing officers when possible.  
* Becoming familiar with chapter goals and ongoing initiatives.  
* Learning organizational policies and procedures.  
* Asking questions whenever clarification is needed.

Successful transitions rely on communication and collaboration between both leadership teams.

# **Best Practices**

Successful officer teams often demonstrate the following practices:

### **Establish Clear Goals**

Set realistic goals for the academic year and review progress regularly. Shared goals help officers stay focused and measure the chapter's success over time.

---

### **Maintain Regular Communication**

Consistent communication among officers, members, the Faculty Advisor, and Headquarters helps ensure everyone remains informed and aligned.

---

### **Delegate Responsibilities**

Sharing responsibilities among officers and committees allows projects to be completed more effectively while providing leadership opportunities for additional members.

---

### **Stay Organized**

Maintain accurate records, calendars, meeting notes, and planning documents to support efficient chapter operations and smooth leadership transitions.

---

### **Encourage Member Participation**

Create opportunities for members to contribute ideas, volunteer for projects, and become involved in chapter activities. Engaged members strengthen the chapter and help develop future leaders.

---

# **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

### **Trying to Do Everything Alone**

Strong chapters are built through teamwork. Officers should delegate responsibilities and work collaboratively rather than relying on a single individual to manage every task.

---

### **Inconsistent Communication**

Failing to share important updates or respond to messages promptly can create confusion and delay chapter activities. Regular communication helps keep projects on track.

---

### **Waiting Until the Last Minute**

Planning events, submitting required documentation, and completing organizational responsibilities early provides time to address questions or unexpected challenges.

---

### **Poor Recordkeeping**

Missing meeting notes, incomplete financial records, or disorganized files can make chapter operations and leadership transitions more difficult.

Maintaining organized documentation supports long-term chapter success.

---

### **Losing Focus on the Mission**

Every meeting, project, partnership, and event should support the mission of Ethos Sustainability. Officers should regularly consider how their decisions contribute to sustainability, entrepreneurship, business, and STEAM education.
